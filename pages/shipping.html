Shipping Information

Clear delivery options before you place your order

Shipping costs are shown during checkout, and the final order summary includes the delivery method you selected. After ordering, keep your order ID so you can follow progress on the tracking page.

Standard Delivery

Estimated arrival: 3 to 5 business days. This is the best option for routine orders when speed is not urgent.

Express Delivery

Estimated arrival: 1 to 2 business days. Recommended when you need your order more quickly.

Overnight Option

Available at checkout when you want the fastest delivery method offered by the store.

After You Order

  • Your order is saved immediately after checkout.
  • You receive an order ID that can be used on the tracking page.
  • Status updates move from pending to delivered as the order progresses.

Important

Delivery timelines are estimates and can vary based on order volume, destination, and confirmation speed for payment-based orders.
